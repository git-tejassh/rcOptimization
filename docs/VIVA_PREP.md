# Viva preparation: likely examiner questions with evidence-backed answers

1. **Why a surrogate if one FE solve takes only 28 ms?** It may not be worth it, and the project measures that: speed-up 3.0x for the optimisation alone and 0.81x including data generation and training (`compute_comparison.json`). The brief asked for a surrogate; the direct-FE baseline is the honest test. The surrogate would matter for a larger model (more storeys, nonlinear or seismic load cases).
2. **Why not seismic?** Brief s5 defers it; the optimum is gravity-only. The STAAD report shows 57 of 133 columns governed by seismic cases, so the optimum is not a compliant design (`docs/SEISMIC_EXTENSION.md`).
3. **Why is the front narrow?** Cost and carbon both follow material volume (r = 0.985 over the feasible dataset). Only small trade-offs exist (a few per cent in cost and carbon), so the Pareto front proper is only 3-7 points (criterion S1 not met); the finding is reported, and rates were not distorted to widen it.
4. **How do you know the IS checks are right?** Anchor tests (Mu,lim/bd2 2.76 / 3.45 / 4.14, Tables 19-20 spot values), independent hand calculations (`docs/hand_calcs`), and STAAD's own column-81 design numbers reproduced (Puz 2901.43 kN, Muz1 150.87 kN.m within 1.5 %, IR 0.39). The IS table transcriptions are still marked provisional until you check them against the printed code.
5. **How do you know the FE is right?** Closed-form beam tests and equilibrium checks, and a benchmark against your STAAD model: reactions to 0.0012 %, column axial force 0.0035 %, column moments 0.12 % (`docs/BENCHMARK.md`).
6. **Why OpenSees and not STAAD in the loop?** Brief s7: STAAD needs a licence, is slow to drive, and cannot run on any OS. STAAD is used as geometry source and benchmark only. The legacy STAAD-driven optimiser was archived because it had fixed demands and a single-point front (`docs/LEGACY_AUDIT.md`).
7. **Is the surrogate reliable near the feasibility boundary?** Boundary U_flex MAE A 0.0213, B 0.0009; feasibility agreement near the boundary A 94.7 %, B 99.8 %. 8. **What did active learning achieve?** 500 boundary-targeted designs; boundary MAE changes quoted in the report, section 4. One round only (brief).
9. **Why two surrogates?** FE demands depend only on sizes and fck; capacity is closed-form. B learns only demands (D2). It is more accurate on U_flex.
10. **Are the cost and carbon numbers real?** No: every rate and factor is provisional (`docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md`, `docs/CARBON_DATA_AUDIT.md`); the dashboard shows a permanent banner. Replace them in `config/*.yaml` and re-run.
11. **What does repair do to the optimiser?** It maps any genome to the nearest buildable IS-standard design; 96.6 % of raw uniform draws were unbuildable. No continuous relaxation is reported as a design.
12. **Is the NSGA-II converged, are results seed-independent?** 5 seeds per configuration, hypervolume against a fixed pooled reference with mean and std (`optimization_summary.json`, dashboard "Compute comparison").
13. **How robust is the front to input uncertainty?** Under +/-20 % changes of steel cost, cement carbon and steel carbon, the baseline cost/carbon optima lose at most 1.0 % against the best design available under each scenario, and the worst rank correlation of cost/carbon across the baseline set is 0.488. The optimum or the ordering is NOT fully robust to these perturbations: see the scenario table.
14. **What is novel?** Nothing algorithmic; NSGA-II for RC frames is published since 2016 (Kaveh et al.). The contribution is a validated, India-contextualised application with a measured surrogate reliability.
15. **Why 0.35 Ig / 0.70 Ig?** Common cracked-section practice; deflection uses the same factors (about 2.9x gross-section deflection, comparable to the IS 456 creep factor 1 + 1.6). Not validated by STAAD (uncracked): flagged in ASSUMPTIONS B6.
16. **What would you do next?** Replace provisional data, add seismic cases and IS 13920 detailing (the hook exists), T-beam action and curtailment, and test on a larger structure where FE cost makes the surrogate pay.
